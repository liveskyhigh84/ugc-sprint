Launch checklist

Eight items, in the order to actually do them. Two are real blockers (nothing ships without them); the rest are quick but not optional.

The single real blocker. Nothing in the current pipeline (Prism/generate, UGC_Studio) can produce a talking avatar today.
Blocker

Two real options, priced as of this week:

OptionCostNotes

Overall: 53.2 → Grade C. Normal for a pre-deployment page with placeholder proof and dead payment buttons. The fixes are already listed above, not new findings.

Why conversion scored only 60, not higher

The structure is strong: sticky order card, guarantee, real scarcity language, FAQ addressing the actual objections (is this really AI, can I run it as a paid ad, why only 5 orders). What's dragging the score down is entirely mechanical: the CTA buttons don't go anywhere yet. Fix item 4 and this score moves on its own.

Why SEO and Growth & Trust stay low regardless

Both are structurally impossible to fix before deployment and before a first sale exists. Not a writing problem: don't spend more copy effort chasing these two scores this week.

The week-1 plan (see ~/marketing-plans/ugc-sprint/final_plan.md) covered the minimum to validate. This expands it for revenue: more channels, a real pricing ladder instead of one SKU, and a retention cadence with actual timing.
