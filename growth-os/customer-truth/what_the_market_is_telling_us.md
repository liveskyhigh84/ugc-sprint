# What the market is telling us — 24-Hour UGC Sprint

Status: pre-first-sale. No sales calls, support tickets, or churn data exist yet (zero orders
as of 2026-10-09). Everything below is sourced from competitor pricing research and public
UGC-buyer complaint language gathered pre-launch — not from this business's own customers.
Do not treat this as customer feedback. Replace/extend each section with real buyer language
the moment it exists (first brief, first refund request, first Sprint Pass conversation).

## Confirmed pain language (sourced, not invented)

- **Credit-waste beats "turnaround" as the sharpest objection.** Real buyer-complaint pattern:
  *"got a sub for a month, used 10 credits in like 15mins, canceled sub same day."* Source:
  live web search of r/AI_UGC_Marketing and adjacent UGC-buyer communities, logged in
  `../../research/ugc_mastery_notes.md` section 1 and `../../marketing/outbound_scripts.md`
  ICP note (2026-09-03).
- **Recurring phrasing across UGC-buyer reviews**: "overpriced, slow, and inconsistent,"
  "retainers lock you in before you know if the hooks even work," "per-video pricing kills
  testing velocity." Same source as above.
- **Implication for the pitch**: flat $297 + refund-if-it-doesn't-beat-what's-running answers
  the credit-waste objection directly. Lead with "you don't burn budget on renders that don't
  work," not just speed — this was already acted on in the shipped `outbound_scripts.md` Reddit
  post draft.

## Outbound reply-rate benchmarks (sourced, §3)

- Generic cold DM: <1% reply. Lightly personalized: ~8%. Deeply personalized: 12-28%.
- Tight lists under 50 recipients: 5.8% average reply vs. 2.1% for 500+ blasts.
- **Implication**: Facebook Ad Library outbound must name the specific ad running, lead with a
  critique before the offer, and stay under ~20 sends/day/account. Already encoded as a rule in
  `../../marketing/outbound_scripts.md`.
