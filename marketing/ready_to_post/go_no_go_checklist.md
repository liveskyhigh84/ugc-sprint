# Go/No-Go Checklist for UGC Sprint Launch

## Pre-Posting Verification

### Reddit Rules Compliance
- [ ] r/SideProject rules checked and understood
- [ ] r/EntrepreneurRideAlong rules checked and understood
- [ ] Content complies with both subreddits' guidelines
- [ ] No explicit self-promotion violations detected
- [ ] Content shows actual progress (not just "just an idea")
- [ ] No repeated submissions of same content in short window

### Content Quality
- [ ] All copy passes AI-tells-validator check
- [ ] Reddit body corrected to avoid false positive on lowercase-after-period
- [ ] All claims are verifiable and not fabricated
- [ ] Video URL confirmed to be accessible
- [ ] Links are properly formatted and functional

### Legal & Safety
- [ ] No false claims about performance or results
- [ ] No personal data or confidential information shared
- [ ] Content adheres to Reddit's global rules
- [ ] No harassment, hate speech, or harmful content
- [ ] All statements made in good faith

### Technical Verification
- [ ] Video at https://liveskyhigh84.github.io/ugc-sprint/generate-out/tape001-final.mp4 loads successfully
- [ ] Landing page (index.html) is accessible and functional
- [ ] All referenced assets exist and are accessible
- [ ] Tracking parameters (if any) are correctly formatted

## Final Go/No-Go Decision

### GO Criteria (All must be checked)
- [ ] Reddit rules compliance verified
- [ ] Content quality validated
- [ ] Legal and safety checks passed
- [ ] Technical verification successful
- [ ] No outstanding issues or concerns

### NO-GO Criteria (Any unchecked prevents launch)
- [ ] Reddit rules violation detected
- [ ] AI-tells-validator shows unresolved issues
- [ ] Legal/safety concerns identified
- [ ] Technical failures preventing access to content
- [ ] Any unresolved concerns from team review
